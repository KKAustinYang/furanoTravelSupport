Method

Made for the mirror, not the feed.

The faceIt stays your face

Only the hair region is regenerated. Skin, jawline, neck and background are composited back from the original pixels — so nobody walks away with a slimmer face they never asked for.

The rangeBig cuts hold together

Long to pixie is where most tools fall apart. A two-stage pass resets the hair before building the target shape, which is what keeps a drastic change believable.

The bookYour own work, on screen

Not a fixed preset list. Your own style photography becomes the reference library, so what a client sees on screen is the cut this salon actually delivers.

Details

Good to know

Where do the photos go?

A photo is processed for the preview and is never kept as a face template. Nothing outlives the session unless the salon turns saving on.

Can we post these to social?

Treat them as consultation aids, not portfolio work. Every image carries an AI-simulation mark, and some listing sites do not accept AI-generated photos as style shots.

How close is it to the real cut?

Close on silhouette and length, looser on texture. Density, curl pattern and condition all move the outcome — the preview starts the conversation, it does not promise the result.

What does setup take?

A tablet at the station and the style library. Reference shots can come straight from your existing gallery, so most of the work is choosing which cuts to offer.
